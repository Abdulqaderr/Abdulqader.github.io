Telecom Customer Analytics

A portfolio-safe, synthetic analytics project that turns customer, plan, usage, billing, payment, and support data into actionable growth and retention insights.

SQLBigQueryPythonPower BI

This case study uses synthetic data and does not contain employer or customer information.

Business problem

Commercial and service teams need one trusted view of subscriber health, revenue, plan performance, and early churn signals.

Data model

Customers and plans connect to subscriptions, usage, invoices, payments, and support tickets through governed identifiers and date dimensions.

Analytical flow

Source data→BigQuery staging→SQL models→KPI marts→Power BI

Questions answered

  • Monthly active users and revenue trend
  • Customer churn and retention cohorts
  • ARPU by segment and plan
  • Top three plans each month
  • High-value customer segments
  • Customers with usage decline above 30%
  • Payment behavior and overdue invoices
  • Support demand by customer segment

Example SQL pattern

WITH monthly_usage AS ( SELECT customer_id, DATE_TRUNC(usage_date, MONTH) AS month, SUM(data_gb) AS usage_gb FROM analytics.fact_usage GROUP BY 1, 2 ) SELECT *, LAG(usage_gb) OVER (PARTITION BY customer_id ORDER BY month) AS prior_usage FROM monthly_usage;

Outcome

A reusable semantic layer and dashboard design that gives business teams consistent definitions, faster analysis, and clear customer-risk signals.
